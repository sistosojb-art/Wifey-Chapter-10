01 · WELCOME TO OUR LITTLE WORLD

Dear my Wifey,

A little place
just for you.

I wanted to make you something you could open whenever you want to feel loved.

This isn't just a website or a collection of pretty pages. It's my little scrapbook for you — a place for words I want you to keep, memories I never want to lose, and feelings that are sometimes difficult to fit into an ordinary message.

Take your time as you go through it. You don't have to read everything all at once. I hope every page reminds you that you are loved, appreciated, and special to me. Some pages may make you smile, some may make you pause, and some are simply here to remind you that someone is thinking of you with a grateful heart.

I hope this becomes a place you can return to, even years from now. You can open it on an ordinary day, on a difficult day, or whenever you want to revisit a little piece of us.

02 · BEFORE US

Two stories, then you came into my life

Before there was an us...

Before we knew each other this way, we were living our own days, carrying our own stories, learning things, making mistakes, and becoming the people we are now.

Neither of us knew exactly where life would lead. There were ordinary moments that probably didn't seem important at the time, but every little part of the journey brought us closer to the day our paths crossed.

I can't rewrite the chapters that came before us, and I don't need to. They helped shape the woman I get to know today and the man I am still working to become.

What matters to me is what we choose to build from here: something honest, gentle, faithful, patient, and centered on what is right.

Then, somehow, there was you. ♡

03 · THE DAY YOU CAME

A new chapter began

You became someone special.

There is something lovely about realizing that a person who once felt new can slowly become part of your everyday thoughts. A message, a laugh, a little update, or even a simple good morning can become something you look forward to.

I don't want to pretend that every moment has been perfect. Our story is real, and real stories have things to learn, conversations to have, and days that ask more from us. But I am grateful for the moments that brought us here.

Thank you for letting me know you, for sharing pieces of your world, and for giving me memories that I can look back on with a smile.

I'm glad our paths crossed, Wifey. ♡

12 · GROWING TOGETHER WITH GOD

✧

A prayer for us

Let's keep God at the center.

More than simply wanting a happy relationship, I want us to keep growing into better people. I want our faith to guide the way we speak, the way we make choices, and the way we treat one another.

May we keep seeking God's wisdom, choosing what is right, and encouraging each other to grow closer to Him. When life gets confusing, may we remember to pray, be patient, and seek His truth instead of letting fear lead us.

“Let all that you do be done in love.”1 Corinthians 16:14

I hope we encourage each other without trying to control each other, and remember that our relationship should never take the place of our relationship with God. I want us to keep choosing kindness, honesty, faithfulness, and patience.

A little prayer corner

Lord, please guide us to grow in wisdom, patience, honesty, and love. Help us make choices that honor You, care for each other with gentleness, and keep You at the center of our lives. Amen.

✿

One last letter, Wifey ko

If I had to choose again...

If I had to choose again, through every chapter, every difficult day, every little moment, and every new beginning, I would still want to grow with you.

Whenever you return to this little world, I hope it reminds you that you are appreciated, that your dreams matter, and that love should be shown through the way we treat one another every day.
